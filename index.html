Local Tips & Spots

💰
Currency & Money
Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). $1 USD ≈ 16,000 IDR. Carry cash for local warungs, temples, and markets. ATMs widely available. Notify your bank before travel. Avoid exchange booths offering "too good" rates.
🛺
Getting Around
Use Grab or Gojek apps for reliable, metered rides. Avoid unmetered taxis. Rent a scooter only if you're experienced. Traffic in Kuta/Seminyak can be chaotic — budget extra time.
🍜
Food & Water
Stick to bottled water — don't drink tap. Try nasi goreng, mie goreng, satay, and babi guling. Warung local spots are cheaper and often delicious. Avoid salads at local spots if worried about stomach.
🛕
Temple Etiquette
Wear a sarong & sash to enter temples (usually rented at entrance). Cover shoulders and knees. Don't enter if menstruating. Remove shoes when entering. Respect ceremonies and don't interrupt.
🌺
Must-See Near Nusa Dua
Uluwatu Temple at sunset, Tanah Lot, Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) Cultural Park, Jimbaran Bay seafood at sunset, Seminyak beach clubs (Ku De Ta, Potato Head), Tegallalang rice terraces.
🩺
Health & Safety
Bali is generally safe. Watch your belongings in busy markets. Drink only bottled water. Bring travel insurance. BIMC Hospital Nusa Dua is the closest quality medical facility to Grand Hyatt. Emergency: 112.
📡
Connectivity
Buy a local SIM at the airport (Telkomsel or XL Axiata) for ~$5-10 for data. Grand Hyatt has excellent WiFi. Download Google Maps offline before you go. WhatsApp works great for communication.
🕐
Time Zone
Bali is WITA — UTC+8. Denver (MDT) is UTC-6, so Bali is 14 hours ahead. When it's 8 AM in Bali, it's 6 PM the previous day in Denver. Plan calls home accordingly!
